| 5.1 Entity Relationship Model — đoạn dẫn | ĐIỀN MỤC TRỐNG | Proposed Entity Relationship Model: Location references Category; LocationTranslation references Location and Language; GuideScript references Location and Language; AudioAsset references GuideScript; a published GuideScript references ContentPackage by packageVersion; Feedback may reference Location. AnalyticsEvent stores location and language identifiers for the external analytics data consumed by UC-07. ERD link: [nhóm điền]. | S08, sheet 6, D12 |
| 5.1 — cuối đoạn mô hình | THÊM CÂU | Package versions are assigned at publication time. Unpublished DRAFT or PENDING_REVIEW scripts do not require packageVersion. The manifest associates the affected location data, scripts, and available audio with their package version for atomic installation. Package-version uniqueness and package grouping by area or language require team confirmation before finalizing database keys and cardinalities. | G33, N03, M09, D04 |
| 5.2 — đoạn dẫn Entity Definitions | ĐIỀN MỤC TRỐNG | Proposed Entity Definitions: The following records are derived from the existing use cases and the data-model worksheet. They describe required information and external references; they do not add account-management, configuration-management, feedback-submission, or event-capture use cases. | S08, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — Location | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: Location. Fields: locationId (proposed PK), categoryId (FK), latitude, longitude, geofenceRadius, thumbnailUrl, status, version. Used by: UC-01, UC-04, UC-05, UC-06. Purpose: location information and its content status; the record version supports concurrent-edit detection and is distinct from packageVersion. | M08, M09, G31, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — LocationTranslation | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: LocationTranslation. Fields: locationId (FK), languageCode (FK), name, description. Used by: UC-01, UC-04, UC-05, UC-06. Purpose: localized names and optional descriptions. Proposed submission rule: at least one name in VI or EN; Save Draft may be incomplete. | M08, D08, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — Category | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: Category. Fields: categoryId (proposed PK), localized name, isActive. Used by: UC-01, UC-06. Purpose: the fixed MVP category catalog; catalog entries and their localized values are [nhóm điền]. No category-management use case is added. | M14, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — Language | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: Language. Fields: languageCode (proposed PK), name, isUiSupported, isContentSupported. Used by: UC-02, UC-03, UC-06. Purpose: the fixed MVP language catalog. Actual support flags and expected device TTS availability are documented in the Appendix; support is not assumed solely because a fallback language is named. | G09, M14, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — GuideScript | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: GuideScript. Fields: scriptId (proposed PK), locationId (FK), languageCode (FK), scriptType (FULL or SHORT), scriptText, status, packageVersion (not required before publication), reviewerComment, updatedBy, updatedAt. Used by: UC-02, UC-03, UC-04, UC-06. Purpose: the guide text for a location, language, and script type, with external review information and published-package association. | M07, M10, G33, G36, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — AudioAsset | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: AudioAsset. Fields: audioId (proposed PK), scriptId (FK), fileUrl, format, durationSec, sizeBytes, checksum. Used by: UC-03, UC-04, UC-06. Purpose: optional audio linked to the script's locationId, languageCode, and scriptType. Proposed limits are defined in UC-06 BR7. | M07, G35, D08, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — ContentPackage | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: ContentPackage. Fields: packageVersion (proposed PK, subject to uniqueness confirmation), scopeArea, languageCode (nullable), sizeBytes, checksum, signature, publishedAt, manifest. Used by: UC-04; publication is external. Purpose: verified atomic installation of affected location data, scripts, available audio, and removed or archived-item visibility changes. | M09, G16, G20, G21, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — LocalContentVersion | THÊM DÒNG | Record: LocalContentVersion, stored on the device. Fields: installedPackageVersion, installedAt, isBase. Used by: UC-04. Purpose: track installed content and distinguish protected Base Content from Optional Content. | sheet 6, D03 |
| 5.2 — Feedback | THÊM DÒNG | Entity: Feedback. Fields: feedbackId (proposed PK), locationId (nullable FK), category, rating (proposed [1–5]), content, languageCode, status (NEW, IN_REVIEW, RESOLVED), createdAt, updatedAt. Used by: UC-07. Supplied through the external feedback-submission dependency; RESOLVED is final. | G40, G42, D09, D11, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — AnalyticsEvent | THÊM DÒNG | Record: AnalyticsEvent. Fields: eventId (proposed PK), eventType, locationId, languageCode, scriptType, source, connectivity, anonymousDeviceId, occurredAt. Used by: UC-07. Supplied through external Analytics Event Capture. The event-contract field timestamp denotes the occurrence time stored as occurredAt, not an additional event time. Event-type applicability is defined in the Appendix. | G38, G39, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — AdminUser | THÊM DÒNG | External reference: AdminUser. Fields: adminId, role (CONTENT_ADMIN or SYSTEM_ADMIN). Used by: UC-06, UC-07. Purpose: refer to the authenticated actor and permissions provided by Admin Authentication; account management remains out of scope. | G44, D09, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — SystemConfig | THÊM DÒNG | Configuration record: SystemConfig. Fields: key, value. Purpose: fixed MVP values such as defaultSearchRadius and notificationCooldown and references to the fixed language and category catalogs. Changes require a release; no configuration-administration use case or editable dashboard is implied. | M14, sheet 6 |
| 5.2 — cuối bảng | THÊM CÂU | Audit information includes createdBy, updatedBy, creation and update timestamps, and content-status history as required by UC-06 BR6. The physical storage design for this history is [nhóm điền]; no additional audit entity or database structure is assumed here. | G32, S08 |

Bảng Entity Definitions mới dùng Entity / Fields / Used by / Purpose; các dòng trên có thể tách vào các cột tương ứng. Chưa có dữ liệu xác nhận khóa tổng hợp của LocationTranslation, tính duy nhất toàn cục của packageVersion hoặc cấu trúc storage của manifest/audit. Đây là điểm nhóm hoàn thiện, không tự thêm entity và ràng buộc.
